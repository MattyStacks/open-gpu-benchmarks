  driver: "561.09"
proof:
  raw_log: raw/rtx_4070_super/cyberpunk_1440p.csv

Review checklist

Required

  • GPU and form factor
  • Game, resolution, and settings
  • Avg FPS and 1% low
  • Frame generation off
  • Evidence reference

Strongly encouraged

  • Raw PresentMon capture
  • Laptop TGP or GPU power
  • Exact card or device name
  • CPU, memory, and power mode
  • Driver and display mode

Outlier check: a submission more than 50% away from a matching official baseline must be corrected, explained by a different comparison profile, or removed during review.

Desktop and laptop GPUs never share a group. Game version remains as metadata, while matching summaries use GPU, form factor, known power profile, game, resolution, and graphics settings.
